As we wrap up another remarkable year at Labs64 NetLicensing, it’s the perfect moment to reflect on our journey and celebrate our achievements. 2024 has been a standout year in our mission to deliver a more powerful, versatile, and customer-centric licensing platform. We introduced groundbreaking features designed to help businesses optimize license management and discover new revenue streams.

Throughout the year, NetLicensing rolled out a variety of impactful updates, enhancing license management, payment integrations, notification systems, and overall platform performance.

Let’s take a closer look at the highlights and key releases that defined 2024.

## Enhanced Licensing Management

### License Bundles

The ability to group models and packages into a single license bundle simplifies complex licensing scenarios. This feature enables businesses to offer multiple products or services under one license agreement, reducing administrative overhead and providing customers with a more streamlined and flexible licensing experience.

![License Bundles](/img/blog/01-netlicensing2024-bundles.png "License Bundles"){:class="blog-center"}

### Backup and Restore

Users can now back up their entire product configuration as a downloadable file and restore it when needed. This ensures business continuity by allowing quick recovery in case of system failures or migration to new environments. It also eliminates the risk of data loss during updates or changes to the licensing setup.  

![Backup and Restore](/img/blog/02-netlicensing2024-backup-restore.png "Backup and Restore"){:class="blog-center"}

## Improved NetLicensing Shop Experience

### Updated Payment Integrations

PayPal and Stripe API integrations have been updated to align with the latest standards, improving compatibility with global payment systems. These updates ensure smoother transactions while supporting a wider range of payment methods.

![Payment Integrations](/img/blog/03-netlicensing2024-payment-integrations.png "Payment Integrations"){:class="blog-center"}

### Recurring Payments

With Stripe integration, recurring payments are now supported for seamless subscription management. This feature automates subscription renewals, reducing manual intervention and ensuring uninterrupted access for customers while improving cash flow predictability for businesses.  

### Recurring Payments for Bundles

Extending recurring payment support to license bundles further streamlines subscription offerings. Businesses can now offer grouped licenses with automated renewals, simplifying billing processes for both providers and customers while enhancing the overall subscription experience.  

### Shop Token Enhancements

Shop tokens now support bundle acquisitions, making it easier for customers to purchase multiple licenses in a single transaction. This improvement enhances the purchasing experience by reducing complexity during checkout and enabling smoother multi-license acquisitions.  

![Shop Token Enhancements](/img/blog/04-netlicensing2024-bundle-token.png "Shop Token Enhancements"){:class="blog-center"}

### Refined NetLicensing Shop Features

The shop interface has been refined to enhance usability, making it easier for customers to navigate, select products, and complete purchases efficiently. These improvements contribute to a more intuitive and satisfying user experience.

## Notifications and Compliance

### Warning Level Notifications

This feature provides real-time alerts when customer warning levels change, allowing businesses to monitor license usage and customer behavior more effectively. By receiving timely notifications, companies can address potential issues proactively, such as usage nearing limits or subscription expirations, ensuring uninterrupted service and improved customer satisfaction.  

![Warning Level Notifications](/img/blog/05-netlicensing2024-notification-warninglevel.png "Warning Level Notifications"){:class="blog-center"}

### Notification Payload Updates

Notification payloads have been restructured to support seamless integration with ElasticSearch and Splunk. This ensures compatibility with modern monitoring tools, allowing businesses to analyze notification data effectively for better insights into system events and customer behavior.  

![Notification Payload Updates](/img/blog/06-netlicensing2024-notification-payload.png "Notification Payload Updates"){:class="blog-center"}

### Configuration Rules for Warning Levels

Businesses can now define custom configuration rules for warning levels, providing greater control over how customer status changes are monitored and managed. This flexibility allows organizations to tailor warning thresholds based on specific operational needs.

![Configuration Rules for Warning Levels](/img/blog/07-netlicensing2024-configuration-rules-warning-levels.png "Configuration Rules for Warning Levels"){:class="blog-center"}

## Documentation and Support

### Updated Documentation

NetLicensing documentation has been updated to reflect new features such as subscription grace periods, notification updates, and recurring payments. These enhancements provide clearer guidance for users integrating NetLicensing into their workflows or leveraging advanced functionalities.

### Community Engagement

Ongoing discussions on platforms like GitHub continue to provide valuable integration guidelines, best practices, and troubleshooting tips for users seeking deeper insights into NetLicensing’s functionality.

![Community Engagement](/img/blog/08-netlicensing2024-community-engagement.png "Community Engagement"){:class="blog-center"}

## Other Interesting Launches

In addition to the top releases mentioned above, we introduced several other features designed to meet user demands and expectations, further enriching NetLicensing’s capabilities.

### Management Console Optimization

The user interface has been optimized by eliminating unnecessary resource loading on every page, resulting in faster load times and a smoother user experience across the platform. This improvement significantly enhances usability for both administrators and customers.  

### Custom Properties in Offline Validation

Offline validation results now include licensee-specific custom properties, enabling businesses to access more detailed and relevant licensing data even without an internet connection. This enhancement improves the accuracy of offline validations and supports better decision-making in disconnected environments.

### Codebase Upgrade

The entire platform has been upgraded to a new technology stack with the latest library versions, ensuring improved performance, enhanced security, and better maintainability. This upgrade future-proofs the platform while delivering faster response times and greater reliability.  

### Client Libraries Revamp

All NetLicensing client libraries have been updated to meet modern security standards and improve functionality. These updates ensure seamless integration with third-party systems while enhancing overall service reliability for developers and end-users alike.
