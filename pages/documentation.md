<div id="usage-scenarios" class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h2 class="col-md-12">Five Ways to Integrate NetLicensing</h2>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">Typical integration patterns, from online validation to offline sites and existing shops. Pick the one closest to your product, or combine them.</p>
</div>

<div class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h3 class="col-md-12">Online validation & activation</h3>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">Your product validates and activates licenses online against the NetLicensing cloud service (Licensing-as-a-Service), and customers can buy licenses online right away.</p>
    <figure>
        <div class="image">
            <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="{{ '/img/usage-scenarios/netlicensing-online.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}" alt="NetLicensing Use Case: Online validation & activation" width="70%">
        </div>
    </figure>
</div>

<div class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h3 class="col-md-12">Offline validation with the NetLicensing Agent on your customer’s premises</h3>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">Some sites cannot reach the NetLicensing cloud service, for example without internet access or because of security rules. The <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/netlicensing-agent">NetLicensing Agent</a> is a standalone module that runs in the local network or on a local host and answers validation requests the same way the cloud service does.</p>
    <figure>
        <div class="image">
            <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="{{ '/img/usage-scenarios/netlicensing-agent-customer.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}" alt="NetLicensing Use Case: Offline validation using NetLicensing Agent on customers’ premises" width="70%">
        </div>
    </figure>
</div>

<div class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h3 class="col-md-12">Offline validation with a license file or a cached response</h3>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">Common for mobile apps and IoT devices that should send fewer validation requests. Share an <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/offline-license-validation">offline validation file</a> with the customer, or cache a successful validation response in the product and reuse it until its <code>ttl</code> passes.</p>
    <figure>
        <div class="image">
            <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="{{ '/img/usage-scenarios/netlicensing-offline-ttl-cache.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}" alt="NetLicensing Use Case: Offline validation using TTL & Cache" width="70%">
        </div>
    </figure>
</div>

<div class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h3 class="col-md-12">Selling through an existing shop or billing platform</h3>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">You can keep your current sales channel. Webhooks from your shop or <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/billing-platforms">billing platform</a> create, extend or deactivate licenses through the REST API, and FastSpring can also connect through the open-source <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/gateway">NetLicensing Gateway</a>. Every purchase ends up as a license assigned to the customer, ready for validation.</p>
    <figure>
        <div class="image">
            <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="{{ '/img/usage-scenarios/netlicensing-integration-ecommerce.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}" alt="NetLicensing Use Case: 3pp e-commerce integration using NetLicensing Gateway" width="70%">
        </div>
    </figure>
</div>

<div class="row NL_block" style="text-align:center;">
    <h3 class="col-md-12">ERP, CRM and BI integration through the REST API</h3>
    <p class="col-md-8 col-md-offset-2">Read and manage customers, licenses and transactions from your CRM, ERP, CMS or BI tools through the <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/restful-api">REST API</a>, and receive <a href="https://netlicensing.io/wiki/notifications">webhook notifications</a> for new customers, new licenses, shop transactions and warning level changes.</p>
    <figure>
        <div class="image">
            <img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="{{ '/img/usage-scenarios/netlicensing-integration-erp-crm-bi.png' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url }}" alt="NetLicensing Use Case: ERP & CRM integration using NetLicensing RESTful API" width="70%">
        </div>
    </figure>
</div>
